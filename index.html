Choose your opponent

Optional, up to 20 characters. Shown on your board and in the shot history.

MacArthur can play at three ranks. Pick one with the mouse, a tap or the arrow keys, then press Start battle.

Place your fleet

Pick a ship, then click (or tap) a square on your fleet's board to place it. The ship starts at that square and runs right or down. Turn it with the Rotate button, the Shift key or R. Click a placed ship to pick it up again. Keyboard: Tab to the board, arrow keys to move, Enter to place.

    Call your shot

    Type a letter A–J and a number 1–10, or click a square on MacArthur's waters. Keyboard: arrow keys to move, Enter to fire.
