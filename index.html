Place your fleet

Pick a ship, then click (or tap) a square on Your fleet to place it. The ship starts at that square and runs right or down. Turn it with the Rotate button, the Shift key or R. Click a placed ship to pick it up again. Keyboard: Tab to the board, arrow keys to move, Enter to place.

    Call your shot

    Type a letter A–J and a number 1–10, or click a square on MacArthur's waters. Keyboard: arrow keys to move, Enter to fire.
